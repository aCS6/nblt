Discussion (Discuss both views and give your opinion)

Human activity has had a negative impact on plants and animals around the world. Some people think that this cannot be changed, while others believe actions can be taken to bring about a change. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

Human activity has clearly damaged plant and animal life across the world, but people disagree about whether this damage can still be reversed. This essay will discuss both views before giving my opinion.

Those who believe the damage cannot be changed argue that many ecosystems have already suffered permanent harm, with some species becoming extinct and natural habitats destroyed beyond recovery. They believe that continued population growth and industrial development make further environmental damage almost inevitable. For example, certain animal species that have already gone extinct due to habitat loss cannot be brought back, regardless of future conservation efforts.

On the other hand, many people believe meaningful change is still possible if governments, businesses, and individuals take serious action. Measures such as protecting natural habitats, reducing pollution, and supporting conservation programmes have already helped some endangered species recover in certain regions. For instance, strict hunting bans and habitat protection have allowed some previously endangered animal populations to slowly increase in numbers, showing that positive change is achievable with sufficient effort.

In my opinion, while some damage is undoubtedly permanent, this does not mean further harm is unavoidable. Strong environmental policies and greater public awareness can still protect many remaining plant and animal species from future decline.

In conclusion, although certain environmental damage cannot be undone, meaningful action can still prevent further harm. I believe continued effort to protect nature remains both necessary and worthwhile.

Two-Part Question

With increasing populations and ever growing urban centres, many countries are losing their natural beauty spots. What benefits are there to protecting places of natural beauty? How can this be solved?

As populations grow and cities continue to expand, many countries are losing important areas of natural beauty. This essay will discuss the benefits of protecting these areas and suggest possible solutions to prevent further loss.

Protecting natural beauty spots offers several important benefits. Firstly, these areas often provide essential habitats for wildlife, helping maintain biodiversity that supports the wider environment. In addition, natural beauty spots offer valuable recreational and mental health benefits for people, providing peaceful spaces for relaxation away from busy urban life. For example, national parks and protected countryside areas allow city residents to enjoy fresh air and outdoor activities, which can significantly improve overall wellbeing. These areas also often generate income through tourism, benefiting local economies while encouraging conservation efforts.

To address the loss of natural beauty spots, governments could introduce stricter regulations limiting urban development in ecologically or scenically important areas. For instance, designating certain regions as protected land can prevent construction companies from building on valuable natural sites. Encouraging more sustainable urban planning, such as building upward rather than outward in cities, could also help reduce the need to expand into surrounding natural areas.

Raising public awareness about the value of these spaces, alongside stronger legal protection, could further help preserve them for future generations.

In conclusion, protecting natural beauty spots offers significant environmental, health, and economic benefits. Stricter development regulations and sustainable urban planning could help prevent their continued loss.

Problem & Solution

Global warming is one of the biggest threats to our environment. What causes global warming? What solutions are there to this problem?

Global warming remains one of the most serious environmental threats facing the world today. This essay will discuss the main causes of global warming and suggest possible solutions to help address this problem.

One major cause of global warming is the burning of fossil fuels, such as coal, oil, and gas, for energy production and transportation. This process releases large amounts of carbon dioxide into the atmosphere, trapping heat and gradually raising global temperatures. In addition, deforestation contributes significantly to global warming, since trees naturally absorb carbon dioxide, and removing large forested areas reduces the planet's ability to manage these harmful emissions effectively.

To address global warming, governments should invest more heavily in renewable energy sources, such as solar and wind power, to reduce dependence on fossil fuels. For example, countries that have significantly expanded renewable energy infrastructure have successfully reduced their overall carbon emissions in recent years. In addition, promoting reforestation projects and protecting existing forests can help restore the planet's natural ability to absorb carbon dioxide.

Encouraging individuals to reduce personal energy consumption, such as using public transport or improving home energy efficiency, can also contribute meaningfully to reducing overall emissions when adopted widely across society.

In conclusion, global warming is primarily caused by fossil fuel use and deforestation. Expanding renewable energy, protecting forests, and encouraging individual action together offer a practical path toward addressing this urgent global problem.
