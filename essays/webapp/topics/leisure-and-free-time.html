Leisure and Free Time

Two-Part Question

Shopping is the favourite pastime for most young people. Why do you think this is? Do you think they should be encouraged to do some other useful activities?

For many young people today, shopping has become their favourite way to spend free time. This essay will explain why this trend has developed and discuss whether young people should be encouraged to pursue other activities instead.

One key reason shopping has become so popular among young people is the growing influence of social media and advertising. Young people are constantly exposed to images of new clothes, gadgets, and trends online, which encourages them to shop frequently in order to keep up with current fashions. In addition, shopping malls and online stores have made shopping more accessible and enjoyable, often combining it with socialising, since many young people enjoy shopping together with friends as a social activity rather than simply buying items they need. For example, spending an afternoon at a shopping centre with friends has become a common form of entertainment, particularly in cities with limited alternative activities.

While shopping can be an enjoyable and social pastime, I believe young people should be encouraged to pursue other activities as well. Excessive shopping can encourage unnecessary spending and materialistic attitudes, which may not benefit young people in the long run. Instead, activities such as sports, volunteering, or creative hobbies like music and art could provide more lasting personal benefits, including improved physical health, new skills, and a stronger sense of achievement. Schools and communities could help by organising more affordable clubs and activities that offer appealing alternatives to shopping.

In conclusion, shopping has become popular among young people mainly due to social media influence and its social nature. However, I believe encouraging alternative activities, such as sports or creative hobbies, would provide more meaningful long-term benefits than frequent shopping.

Discussion

Having more money and less free time is better than earning less money and having more free time. Discuss both views and state your opinion.

Some people believe it is better to earn a high salary even if this means having very little free time, while others prefer to earn less in exchange for more leisure time. This essay will discuss both views before giving my own opinion.

Those who prioritise money argue that financial security brings significant benefits, such as the ability to afford a comfortable home, good healthcare, and a better standard of living for their family. A higher income also allows people to save for the future and handle unexpected expenses without stress. For many people, especially those supporting a family, earning more money feels like the safer and more responsible choice, even if it requires working long hours and sacrificing personal time.

On the other hand, those who value free time argue that having time for family, hobbies, and relaxation contributes more to overall happiness and wellbeing than money alone. People with more free time often report lower stress levels and better mental health, since they have time to rest and pursue activities they truly enjoy. For example, someone who works fewer hours may have more energy to spend quality time with their children or pursue a hobby that brings them genuine satisfaction, benefits that a higher salary cannot always provide.

In my opinion, while financial stability is important, having enough free time is equally essential for a balanced and healthy life. Money alone cannot buy happiness if a person is constantly stressed or too exhausted to enjoy it.

In conclusion, both money and free time are valuable, but I believe a reasonable balance between the two leads to a more fulfilling life than prioritising one entirely over the other.
