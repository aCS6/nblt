Two-Part Question

Some people fail in school, but end up being successful in life. Why do you think that is the case? What is the most important thing to succeed in life?

It is often observed that people who do not perform well at school later become highly successful in their careers. This essay will explain why this happens and discuss what factor matters most for success in life.

One reason for this is that traditional schooling mainly measures academic ability, such as memorising facts or performing well in exams, which does not reflect all types of intelligence. Some people have strong practical skills, creativity, or business sense that schools do not always recognise or reward. For example, many successful entrepreneurs struggled academically as students but later built thriving businesses because they were skilled at recognising opportunities and taking risks, qualities that exams rarely measure.

In my opinion, the most important factor for success in life is determination. People who are willing to work hard, learn from failure, and keep trying despite setbacks are often more successful than those who simply performed well in school but lack persistence. For instance, many well-known business leaders faced early failures or rejections, yet their determination to keep improving eventually led to significant success, regardless of their academic background.

While natural talent and good education can certainly help, they do not guarantee success without the willingness to work consistently toward a goal.

In conclusion, school performance does not always reflect a person's true abilities, and qualities such as determination and practical skills often matter more. Ultimately, persistence appears to be the most important ingredient for long-term success.

Some people believe that first impressions are the most accurate way to judge someone's character. I disagree with this view, as I believe first impressions are often based on limited and sometimes misleading information.

For this reason, I believe it takes time, repeated interactions, and observing someone's behaviour in different situations to form an accurate judgement of their character.

In conclusion, while first impressions can offer some initial clues, they are frequently inaccurate or incomplete. Judging a person fairly requires more time and a broader understanding of their behaviour.

Advantages & Disadvantages

Some people enjoy change in life, while other people try to avoid it. What are the advantages and disadvantages of both approaches?

People react differently to change in their lives, with some embracing it eagerly while others prefer stability. This essay will discuss the advantages and disadvantages of both approaches.

Those who enjoy change often benefit from new opportunities and personal growth. Trying new experiences, such as changing careers or moving to a new city, can open doors to better opportunities and help people develop new skills. For example, someone who accepts a challenging new job role may struggle at first but often grows more confident and capable as a result. However, constantly seeking change can also create instability, as frequently switching jobs or relationships may prevent people from building long-term expertise or strong, lasting connections with others.

On the other hand, those who avoid change often enjoy greater stability and security in their lives. Sticking to familiar routines, jobs, or relationships can reduce stress and provide a sense of comfort and predictability. For instance, someone who remains in the same job for many years often develops deep expertise and strong professional relationships over time. Nevertheless, avoiding change too strictly can lead to missed opportunities, as such individuals may remain in unsatisfying situations simply because they fear the uncertainty that change brings.

In conclusion, embracing change can lead to growth and new opportunities but may also create instability, while avoiding change offers security at the potential cost of missed opportunities. A balanced approach, open to change when genuinely beneficial, seems most practical.

Advantages & Disadvantages

More and more businesses as well as individuals are choosing to communicate either professionally or socially using technology rather than being face to face. Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of using technology for communicating.

Technology is increasingly being used for both professional and social communication instead of traditional face-to-face interaction. This essay will discuss the advantages and disadvantages of this growing trend.

One major advantage of using technology for communication is convenience. People can now communicate instantly regardless of location, which is especially useful for international businesses coordinating across different time zones. For example, colleagues working in different countries can hold video meetings without the time and expense of travelling, allowing decisions to be made much more quickly. Technology also allows people to maintain social relationships more easily, as messaging apps make it simple to stay connected with friends and family despite busy schedules.

However, there are notable disadvantages as well. Communicating through technology can sometimes lead to misunderstandings, since tone and body language, which help convey meaning clearly, are often lost in text-based messages. For instance, a message intended as a joke may be misinterpreted as rude if the recipient cannot see the sender's tone or facial expression. In addition, relying heavily on technology for social interaction may weaken close personal relationships, as digital communication often lacks the depth and warmth of genuine face-to-face conversation.

Excessive use of technology for communication may also reduce important interpersonal skills, particularly among younger people who grow up relying primarily on digital messaging.

In conclusion, while technology offers valuable convenience and efficiency for communication, it also carries the risk of misunderstandings and weaker personal connections. A balanced approach that includes face-to-face interaction remains important.

Many young children have unsupervised access to the internet and are using the internet to socialise with others. This can lead to a number of dangerous situations which can be threatening for children. What problems do children face when going online without parental supervision? How can these problems be solved?

An increasing number of children now use the internet without adult supervision, which can expose them to serious risks. This essay will discuss the problems children may face online and suggest possible solutions.

One major problem is exposure to inappropriate or harmful content, such as violent material or content unsuitable for their age. Without supervision, children may accidentally or deliberately access websites that are not appropriate for their development. Another serious risk is contact with strangers who may pretend to be someone else in order to gain a child's trust, which can lead to dangerous real-world situations. For example, children using social media without supervision may unknowingly share personal information with strangers, putting their safety at risk.

To address these problems, parents should closely monitor their children's internet use, particularly at a young age, and use parental control software to restrict access to unsuitable websites. In addition, schools could introduce internet safety education, teaching children how to recognise dangerous situations and protect their personal information online. For instance, some schools now run digital literacy programmes that teach students about safe online behaviour from an early age, which has helped reduce risky online activity among students.

Encouraging open communication between parents and children about their online experiences can also help children feel comfortable reporting anything that makes them uncomfortable.

In conclusion, unsupervised internet access exposes children to serious risks such as harmful content and contact with strangers. Parental supervision, combined with internet safety education, offers an effective solution to these problems.

There is ongoing debate about whether intelligence is something people are born with, or whether it can be developed through learning and experience. This essay will discuss both views before giving my opinion.
